Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

Varkanis (“we”, “us”, the “Service”) is a personal gamification web application accessible at apocrypha-ochre.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, display name, and avatar URL from Google.
  • Profile data: content you create in the app (character name, stats, skills, study decks and cards, plans, goals).
  • TikTok data (only if you explicitly connect a TikTok account): your TikTok username and public statistics such as follower count, following count, and total likes. We never see private messages, drafts, or unpublished content.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request timestamps) collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Supabase).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Service: authenticate you, store and display your profile, sync the data you create across devices.
  • To display third-party stats you have explicitly connected (e.g. TikTok follower count on your dashboard).
  • To diagnose technical errors and maintain the Service.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Profile data is stored in Supabase (Postgres database hosted in the United States or European Union, depending on the project region). Authentication tokens are held by Supabase Auth. Static assets are served by Vercel.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data is shared only with the infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the Service (Google OAuth, Supabase, Vercel, TikTok API when connected). When you connect TikTok, you authorise us to fetch the public statistics described above on your behalf, and our use of that data complies with TikTok’s Developer Terms of Service.

5. Retention and Deletion

Your profile is retained while your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will erase your data within 30 days of receiving the request. Disconnecting a third-party integration (e.g. TikTok) immediately revokes our access tokens and stops further data collection from that source.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated within the app.

9. Contact
